# Layer 2–3 visual and document QA

Reviewed 2026-10-13 for release sonar-s7-s10-layer23-20261013-v3.

All 12 final Layer 2–3 PNG diagnostics were reviewed together. Bidirectional original traces (L23_02), the failed local-alignment read (L23_03), inverse sets (L23_09), the S7 anomaly and actual event distributions (L23_10), and held-out predictions (L23_12) were also inspected at full viewing size. Axes, channel colors, candidate-site markers, units, plateaus, unavailable states and uncertainty labels are legible and consistent with the exported data. The full-size figure files, rather than the reduced review sheet, are the analytical artifacts. PNG and editable SVG exports are both included.

These are diagnostic figures, not final publication artwork. The 12-figure evidence inventory records datasets, source tables, observed numbers, scientific messages and limits. No plot claims an absolute RNA-editing fraction or a validated RES/PER. RNA-population uncertainty and n=2 wells per dose remain explicit. Trace panels preserve original dye identity for reverse reads.

The integrated English and Chinese reports, four method reports, blocker register, figure inventory, and source/decision registers were checked for agreement with actual final result tables. Thirteen final report and register files were then regenerated in the isolated second execution directory and compared byte-for-byte. Computational reproducibility is recorded separately in reproducibility_record.json and does not imply independent wet-lab replication.
